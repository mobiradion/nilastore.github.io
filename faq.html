Frequently Asked Questions

🛒 How do I place an order?

Browse our catalog, click on any product to select your desired size or options, and click Add to cart. In your cart, click Checkout, enter your address, and tap Place order on WhatsApp. Your order is sent directly to our team on WhatsApp for instant confirmation.

💳 What payment methods are supported?

We accept UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), and Cash on Delivery (COD).

🚚 How long does delivery take?

Orders in Chennai and Tamil Nadu usually arrive within 1-3 business days. For the rest of India, delivery typically takes 4-6 business days.

📏 How do I check sizing for apparel?

Every fashion product modal includes a dedicated 📏 Size Chart & Measurements button. Click it to view detailed chest, waist, length, and fitting specifications in inches.

🔄 What is your return policy?

We offer a 7-day hassle-free return and exchange window. Simply message us on WhatsApp with your order details and we'll arrange a doorstep pickup.
