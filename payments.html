How WhatsApp Order Payment Works

  1. Cart Checkout: Review your items, enter your delivery address and contact number, and click Place order on WhatsApp.
  2. Order Confirmation: Your order details and total amount are automatically sent to our WhatsApp desk.
  3. Payment Link / Confirmation: Our team shares a secure UPI QR / payment link or confirms Cash on Delivery per your preference.
  4. Instant Dispatch: Once verified, your order is packed and dispatched with live courier tracking.

🔒 100% Payment Protection: All online transactions are processed through RBI-compliant, 256-bit SSL encrypted payment gateways. We never store sensitive banking credentials.
